About

I am an astrophysicist working at the interface between observational astronomy, space missions, and data-intensive survey science. I am involved in international collaborations including the Rubin LSST Solar System Science Collaboration, ESA Comet Interceptor activities, and large spectroscopic surveys.

Cometary science

Detection and characterization of cometary dust activity, with emphasis on long-period comets, interstellar objects, and large-scale survey discovery spaces.

Comet Interceptor mission →

Rubin LSST

Development of automated tools for cometary activity detection and dust-tail characterization in the Vera C. Rubin Observatory LSST era.

Research focus

My current research portfolio combines physically motivated modelling, observational programmes, and software development for next-generation astronomical surveys.

Comets, dust environments, and mission preparation

I contribute to the scientific preparation of cometary missions and observing campaigns by studying dust activity, coma morphology, and the detectability of faint or distant active bodies.

Comet Interceptor Dust modelling Long-period comets

Survey science and automated characterization

Through the Italian Rubin LSST in-kind contribution, I coordinate work on software tools for identifying and characterizing cometary activity in survey images.

Rubin LSST TailKit Solar System surveys

Exoplanet atmospheres and AI methods

I am involved in interdisciplinary projects using machine-learning and data-analysis approaches for astrophysical classification, retrieval, and interpretation problems.

Exoplanets Machine learning Atmospheres

Stellar populations

My broader expertise includes stellar astrophysics and Galactic archaeology, developed through participation in major spectroscopic survey collaborations.
